Enter the World of OREN

A dark supernatural world of nightmares, hunters, hidden bloodlines, strange powers, and a destiny that refuses to stay buried.

Oren

A teenager haunted by nightmares he doesn't understand. Something has been hidden inside him, and the closer he gets to the truth, the more dangerous his world becomes.

The Blood Stone

A mysterious purple-and-red stone connected to Oren's blood and the supernatural power sealed away from him. Some people would kill to possess it.

The Hunter Clan

Hunters walk closer to humanity than the creatures they pursue. Their markings tell stories of what they've killed, and some secrets are written beneath the skin.
